# Native player life and courtyard strikes

This increment belongs to the opt-in local development recipient-combat slice. Ordinary merchant builds and Shipping keep that slice disabled. It does not qualify public hosting, account authentication or MMO capacity.

The retained server character model owns HP, life generation, the last accepted strike, and the ten-second revive deadline. The native scale is 100 HP and 20 damage per courtyard strike. The standalone Blueprint hero keeps its existing 10 raw HP balance and 100–0 display. Native combat never reads the Blueprint HP as its source of life and never routes incoming hits through the template's damage/death graph.

The one stationary native raider chooses the nearest eligible server-owned living pawn within 180 cm. It faces that pawn and displays a 0.70-second strike cue. At impact it checks the same recipient, reciprocal possession, pawn, player generation, enemy life, range, facing and obstruction again. An impact can occur only within the 0.20-second window. A miss or late tick spends the attack and starts 1.60 seconds of recovery. The server supplies the 20 damage; there is no client damage, target, HP or timestamp RPC. Strike IDs survive this enemy's death/respawn. Replacing this server enemy instance would need an issuance epoch before being supported.

At zero HP, outgoing actions use the canonical life gate, attacks cancel, authority movement stops, and the public pawn life component hides the body/weapon and presents the existing original jade spirit mesh. The owning client's existing health and revive UI receives the same state. The model retains health and the deadline when a controller disconnects or its pawn changes; a fresh possessed body is given the retained life before it becomes a valid combatant. Actual independent-client ordering is still a required test.

Revive is a manual intent containing only the displayed life generation. The server rechecks ownership, deadline, pause/teardown and exact dead generation, refuses an occupied server-selected home capsule, and teleports the same possessed body home before committing a candidate life. Successful revive restores 100 HP and increments generation once. No corpse-destruction callback, new character, item, currency or quest payout is involved. Damage and revive remain available while an initialized character's journal transaction is held; progression actions continue to obey journal recovery gates.

Life is retained for this server session only. Persistent progression already uses the independent character journals, but health/death across a server restart and persistent account ownership are future work. Do not claim these are covered by the session ticket or by a headless synthetic authority fixture.

Qualification requires: portable failure/time/overflow tests; native ownership, geometry, movement and unchanged progression tests; two then four independent clients showing damage isolation, peer ghost presentation, duplicate/early/stale revive refusal, disconnected dead-player resume, journal-held damage, and the ordinary merchant/restart regression loop. Rendered art, controls, animation feel, packet timing and packaged behavior need actual gameplay acceptance.

On 12 October 2026 the portable life executable passed seven groups, and the corrected native life fixture passed with zero errors or warnings in a fresh isolated directory. It opened real independent progression journals, exercised native strikes with actual obstruction queries, cancelled a captured target after unpossession, applied five hits from 100 to zero, rebound the retained dead life to a replacement body, refused early/stale/occupied-home/repeated revive, restored movement state once, and kept progression unchanged. The fixture advances normal small world frames and verifies elapsed time; it does not forge the server clock. Its blocked-readiness flag models a held journal gate, rather than injecting a real failed pending write. Its replacement body is trusted ownership assembly, not a disconnect/reconnect transport test. Thirty-one existing native regressions passed in a separate fresh run. Earlier life fixture attempts failed on invalid LocalPlayer ownership, noncanonical character ID and Unreal frame-time clamping; they are not passing results.
